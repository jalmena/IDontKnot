# Structures

Sets of knots tied together into one thing, such as a lashing, which joins two poles with clove hitches, wraps and frapping turns. They will have a section of their own, Structures, open to every discipline.

Until then they wait here, out of the library: the application does not load them and the tests do not check them.

- `square-lashing`: the square lashing, round two poles. Its document (`knot.json`) is made from its drawing (`layout.json`) by `tools/knots/lay.mjs`, as a knot's is, once the folder is under `content/knots`. It is not drawn tight afterwards (`"tightening": false`): a lashing is drawn tight turn by turn as it is tied.
